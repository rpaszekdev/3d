Two Systems of Thought

Kahneman, 2011
System 1

Fast

Automatic, intuitive, always running in the background.

  • Speed Instant, parallel
  • Effort Effortless
  • Control Involuntary
  • Process Pattern matching, association
  • Output Impressions, feelings, intuitions
  • Cost Cheap, biased, often right
Recognizing a friend's face. Reading the word STOP. Sensing anger in a voice. 2 + 2.
System 2

Slow

Deliberate, effortful, allocated attention to mental work.

  • Speed Sequential, serial
  • Effort Demands focus
  • Control Voluntary, deliberate
  • Process Rule-following, computation
  • Output Beliefs, choices, commitments
  • Cost Expensive, lazy, accurate when engaged
17 × 24. Parking in a tight space. Filling out a tax form. Following an unfamiliar route.
