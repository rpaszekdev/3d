`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
Grid-edge boundary violations (X<0, Y<0, Z<0) from shapes centered at the grid origin are a common silent failure mode in voxel renderers — the sphere at `(0,8,0)` with radius 1 clips to X=-1 which is illegal. The safe rule: keep primitive centers at least `radius` away from all grid walls.
