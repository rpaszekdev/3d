## Spatial Reasoning: Camera Visibility Problem

Let me work through this step by step before sketching.

**Camera vector analysis:**
- Camera: `(0,8,0)` → Look-at: `(8,4,8)`
- Direction: `(8, -4, 8)` — camera is looking **right (+X), down (-Y), and deep (+Z)**
- Camera is above-left-front relative to the cube

**Cube bounds:** 4×4×4 at `(6,1,6)` → spans `X:[6,10], Y:[1,5], Z:[6,10]`

**Relative position of camera to cube center `(8,3,8)`:**
- Camera offset: `(-8, +5, -8)` — camera is to the LEFT, ABOVE, and IN FRONT

**Therefore, visible faces:**
1. **Left face** (X=6, facing −X toward camera)
2. **Top face** (Y=5, facing +Y toward camera which is above)
3. **Front face** (Z=6, facing −Z toward camera which is in front)

**Ray intersection check:** Parametric ray `(8t, 8−4t, 8t)`:
- Hits `X=6` at `t=0.75` → Y=5, Z=6 → the **top-left-front corner** of the cube!
- So the look-at point `(8,4,8)` is **inside the cube** — the cube is dead center of view.

**Floor occlusion analysis:**
For floor point `(fx, 0, fz)`, ray is blocked if any `t ∈ [3/8, 7/8]` (Y constraint for cube) gives `6 ≤ t·fx ≤ 10` AND `6 ≤ t·fz ≤ 10`:
- Minimum floor X/Z that gets occluded: `6/(7/8) ≈ 6.86` → integer 7
- Maximum: `10/(3/8) ≈ 26.7` → beyond grid
- **Occluded floor region: roughly X:[7,12], Z:[7,12]** (extended shadow behind cube)

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
The look-at direction ray at `t=0.75` hits exactly `(6,5,6)` — the top-left-front corner of the cube. This is why all THREE visible faces meet at that corner: it's the most "camera-facing" vertex of the cube. This is a key geometric property: the number of visible faces equals the number of coordinate axes along which the camera is offset from the cube.

The floor shadow extends further than the cube's footprint because the camera is elevated — rays from a high camera project a larger shadow. A camera at the same Y as the cube would see zero top face and infinite floor shadow behind it.
