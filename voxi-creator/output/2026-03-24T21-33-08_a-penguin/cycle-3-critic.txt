`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
**Render visibility is a critical pipeline concern in voxel art.** When the dominant body color (`f` = navy) is near-black and the renderer uses a dark/black background, the model becomes entirely invisible — a problem compounded when secondary colors (white belly, orange feet) are too small relative to the body mass. This is a "dark-on-dark" failure mode that affects scoring even before artistic merit is evaluated.
`─────────────────────────────────────────────────`
