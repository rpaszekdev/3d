`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
In voxel art, a "hole" through an object (like a handle) requires explicit **negative space** — the surrounding frame voxels are placed, but the interior cells are left empty. The renderer won't carve holes automatically. The front-view angle is the single most revealing test for handle depth: if the handle appears as a thin line rather than a solid loop, it lacks Z-depth voxels.
