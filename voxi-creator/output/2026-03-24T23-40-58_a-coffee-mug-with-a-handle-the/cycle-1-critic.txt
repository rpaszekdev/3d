`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
When evaluating voxel mugs, the **handle hole** is architecturally the hardest part — it requires a hollow loop, not just an attached block. Builders often achieve this by placing two vertical bars and two horizontal bars, leaving the interior gap empty. The **top opening** of the mug is equally critical and often missed, requiring the cylinder to be built as a hollow shell rather than a solid primitive.
