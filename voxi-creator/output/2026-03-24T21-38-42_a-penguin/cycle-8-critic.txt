`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
This is a classic **color-background collision** bug in voxel art. The renderer uses `#13151f` (near-black dark navy) as its background, so any voxels using color `0` (black) or `f` (navy) will be nearly or completely invisible — they literally blend into the void. This is why the entire penguin disappears: a real penguin's black body is the **worst possible** color choice for this renderer.
