Innovation 01

VOXI — 3D Sketching with Text

A domain-specific language where every line of text creates a 3D shape. LLMs can write it, read it, and iterate on it.

📝 VOXI Code
-- Penguin in 8 lines
V 16 16 16

-- Body
E 8 5 8 3 4 3 0    -- black ellipsoid body
E 8 5 7 2 3 2 8    -- white belly

-- Head
S 8 10 8 2 0       -- black sphere head

-- Eyes
P 7 11 6 8         -- left eye (white)
P 9 11 6 8         -- right eye (white)

-- Beak
B 7 10 5 2 1 1 7   -- orange beak

-- Feet
B 6 0 7 2 1 3 7   -- left foot
B 9 0 7 2 1 3 7   -- right foot
🎯 Key Features

✦ 8 primitive types: Box, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Ellipsoid, Dome, Point, Group

✦ 16 colors with hex notation (0-f)

✦ Groups & instancing: Define once, place many times

✦ Scale command: R 2 doubles everything — resolution-independent

✦ Animation: T N command for keyframes

✦ LLM-native: Both human and AI readable

Created: penguins, castles, floor plans, FPS arenas, terrain with procedural noise — all from text.

Innovation 02

Mind's Eye — AI Sees in Text

Six orthographic depth projections rendered as numbers. The LLM reads depth values (0-9) and reasons about 3D space without any vision model.

The perception loop — no images anywhere

Closed loop: write → see → reason → fix → repeat. All in text.
