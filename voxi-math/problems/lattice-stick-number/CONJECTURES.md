# Conjectures

## Active

- [UNTESTED] **"sL(K) = 2·s(K) for all knots K"** — Lattice stick number equals twice the general stick number. Holds for all three known cases (trefoil, figure-8, 5₂). Geometric intuition: each free stick maps to an L-shaped pair of lattice sticks. First test: build 5₁ with 16 sticks (predicted by conjecture).

- [UNTESTED] **"sL(5₁) = 16"** — The (2,5) torus knot fits in 16 lattice sticks. Known bounds: [16,17]. If our 2x conjecture holds, it's 16.

- [UNTESTED] **"sL(7₁) = 18"** — The (2,7) torus knot fits in 18 lattice sticks. Predicted by 2x conjecture (s(7₁)=9). Known bounds: [18,23].

- [UNTESTED] **"Torus knots T(2,n) have a simple lattice stick formula"** — The pattern for torus knots might be particularly clean on the lattice because of their rotational symmetry.
