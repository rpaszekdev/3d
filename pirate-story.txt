The sun rose over a calm blue ocean. A lone pirate ship drifted slowly near a small tropical island with palm trees and golden sand.

Captain Blackwood stood on the deck, telescope in hand. He spotted three enemy warships approaching from the east, their red flags rippling in the wind.

The battle began. Cannonballs flew through the air as smoke billowed from all four ships. The ocean churned with explosions.

After hours of fighting, only Blackwood's ship remained. The enemy fleet lay in ruins, their masts broken, slowly sinking beneath the waves.

Blackwood sailed his battered ship to the island. His crew found a treasure chest buried under the tallest palm tree, filled with gold coins glittering in the sunset.
